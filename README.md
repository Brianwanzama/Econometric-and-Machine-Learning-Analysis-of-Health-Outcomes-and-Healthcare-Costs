#### Disclaimer
The dataset used in this analysis is entirely synthetic.
The statistical results and machine learning metrics generated are not factually correct and do not represent real‑world clinical or financial data.

#### Objective
The primary objective of this study is to develop and evaluate statistical and machine learning models capable of explaining and predicting individual-level outcome measures using synthetic data. The analysis emphasises model interpretability, robustness, and predictive accuracy within a reproducible research framework.
#### Goal
This project aims to explore and quantify the relationship between outcome variables and key explanatory factors such as demographic characteristics, categorical attributes, and institutional indicators. By examining the direction, magnitude, and stability of estimated effects, the study identifies which variables contribute most strongly to observed variation and how different modelling approaches capture these relationships.

#### Features considered 
- Age: Age of individuals (in years)
- Gender: Binary indicator of sex (e.g., 1 = male, 0 = female)
- Insurance Status: Indicator of insurance coverage or provider category
- Medical Condition: Categorical indicator of underlying condition or diagnosis
- Admission Type: Type of admission (e.g., emergency, urgent, elective)
- Billing Amount: Total monetary charges associated with an individual record
